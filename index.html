Feelsair Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026.

Developer: Martin Baránek

Privacy and support contact: martin25baranek93@gmail.com

This policy applies to Feelsair: Weather Widget for Android (package dev.feelsair.widget). Feelsair displays current and feels-like temperatures on your home screen. It does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. The developer does not operate a server that receives weather requests or location data from the app.

Location and background location

You can choose a fixed city without granting location permission. If you choose to use your location, Feelsair requests Android's approximate location permission. It does not request precise location access.

If you explicitly enable Follow location and grant the separate background location permission, Feelsair accesses approximate location to update your home-screen weather as you move, even when the app is closed or not in use. Android may limit or delay location and weather updates. You can disable Follow location in the app's Location screen or revoke location permission in Android settings at any time.

Coordinates are rounded to two decimal places before being saved or sent to the weather provider. Rounding reduces detail but does not make location anonymous. Feelsair stores the latest selected location and relevant timestamps; it does not deliberately build a location history.

Weather requests and third parties

To retrieve weather, Feelsair sends the rounded coordinates to Open-Meteo over HTTPS. City searches send the text you enter to Open-Meteo's geocoding service. The provider receives your connection IP address and ordinary technical request information, including the app's User-Agent. These requests support weather and city-search functionality. Location is not used by Feelsair for advertising, profiling or sale.

OpenMeteo GmbH operates the weather and geocoding services and states that it acts as an independent controller for processing covered by its privacy notice. Its current free-service policy says server logs may contain IP addresses and geographical coordinates and are deleted after 90 days. The provider controls that processing and retention; deleting data in Feelsair does not delete provider logs. See Open-Meteo's terms and privacy policy for current details and contact information.

Data stored on your device

Private app storage holds the latest selected location, weather readings and timestamps, refresh preferences, location-following preference, per-widget appearance settings and new-widget defaults. Feelsair retains these for app functionality until they are replaced or deleted as described below. There is no account or developer-hosted location database.

Optional background images

Choosing a background image uses Android's file picker. Feelsair does not request access to your entire photo library. The selected image is resized and re-encoded as a JPEG in private app storage; source metadata is not copied to the new JPEG. Image processing and blur happen on your device. Feelsair does not upload these images to Open-Meteo or a developer server. Frosted mode does not read your system wallpaper.

Removing or replacing an image detaches it from that widget, but imported copies may remain in private app storage until that storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Deleting a widget removes its saved style entry; other styles and defaults remain.

Deletion and your choices

In the app's Settings screen, Delete local weather data clears saved weather and location preferences and turns off location following. Widget appearance settings and imported images are kept. Clear Feelsair's storage through Android settings or uninstall it to remove all app-managed local data. Original images you selected through the file picker are not deleted. Android cloud backup is disabled, and app extraction rules exclude private settings and files from backup and device transfer; device behavior may vary.

Because Feelsair has no accounts or developer-hosted user database, there is no account to delete. You can contact the developer using the privacy contact above with questions or requests about personal data, including any correspondence you send. Do not include location details or other sensitive information unless needed for your request. Weather-provider requests should also be directed to that provider when they concern its server logs.

Permissions and security

Internet access and network-state access enable weather requests and scheduling. Optional approximate and background location permissions support the location features described above. The boot-completed permission restores refresh scheduling and location registration after a device restart. Feelsair does not request contacts, microphone, camera, advertising-ID or precise-location permissions.

Weather and geocoding requests use HTTPS. App data and imported images are kept in Android's private app storage. No system can guarantee absolute security; protect access to your device and its backups.

Policy hosting and external links

If you open this policy or an external link in a browser, the hosting provider receives ordinary website connection information separately from the app. This policy is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages, whose handling of website visits is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement. The policy page includes no analytics scripts, advertising, external fonts or tracking cookies added by the developer.

Weather attribution and updates

Weather data is provided by Open-Meteo under CC BY 4.0. City-search data is based on GeoNames through Open-Meteo. Temperatures are model-based estimates, not measurements from your phone.

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes requiring consent will be presented in the app before the changed processing begins.
